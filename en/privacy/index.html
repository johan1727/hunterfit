This notice describes the current version of HunterFit and the choices available to you.

Effective October 10, 2026 · Operator: Jhonatan Villagomez, United States

HunterFit is a wellness, nutrition, and workout app. Its records and AI estimates are not medical advice or diagnoses.

Information we use

  • Account and profile: email and account identifier, username, goals, and preferences you add.
  • Workout and wellness: routines, exercises, activity and steps, food, calories and nutrients logged, weight, measurements, fasting, water, and progress photos you choose to save.
  • Content you submit: food photos, voice used to recognize food, text you enter, and routines you paste or import for AI features.
  • Technical data: information needed for authentication, security, service operations, and diagnostics.

How we use it

We use information to create and secure your account, save and display your records, calculate summaries, sync the app, respond to requests, and provide features you choose.

If you use an AI feature, the content needed for that feature is sent to Google Gemini to produce the requested result. A food photo and optional context are sent for analysis; the result is shown for you to review. Voice is sent for recognition. Progress photos are stored in your account’s private storage and, if you choose an analysis feature, the image is also sent to the AI provider.

Service providers

  • Supabase: authentication, database, server functions, and private storage. The Supabase project used by HunterFit is hosted in Canada.
  • Google Gemini: processes photos, voice, and text when you request AI features. Google handles that content under the terms and settings of the service/API used by HunterFit; its retention and use may depend on that service.
  • Google or Apple: participates if you choose that sign-in provider and the option is available.

Some processing therefore takes place outside the United States. HunterFit does not sell your personal information as part of its current features. The app does not create a food-photo or voice file in its own storage, or add those inputs to your food diary; it saves food entries that you confirm. Progress photos you choose to save remain in your account.

Photos, voice, and retention

Progress photos are stored in a private bucket and can be deleted in the app. Food photos and voice recordings are processed to respond to your request; the app does not save them in its file storage or add them to your diary. The AI provider may apply its own logs and retention periods under the service used.

Account records are kept while your account remains active. When deletion completes successfully, HunterFit records and associated files are removed from active systems. We retain a minimal technical marker — the account identifier and deletion date — to prevent the deleted profile from being recreated. It is used only to block the HunterFit profile and does not change data in other apps. Backups and technical provider logs may take time to expire under provider schedules or legal requirements.

Your choices and requests

You can correct data the app lets you edit, stop using optional AI features, or request access, correction, or deletion. To delete your account, open Profile → Delete account in the app and confirm. You can also call the privacy contact below. If we cannot fulfill a request, you can call us and ask for a review.

Deletion removes HunterFit data and keeps your shared sign-in identity. Data in other apps that use the same sign-in are not deleted.

Children

We do not seek to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, call the privacy contact to request deletion.

Security and changes

We use authentication and access controls and keep progress photos private. No system can guarantee absolute security. We may update this notice when the app changes; the effective date will be shown at the top.
