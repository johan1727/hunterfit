Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile → Delete account, read the confirmation, and continue only if you want to delete HunterFit data. Your shared sign-in and MY EX data remain intact. It may take a few moments. Associated records and files are removed from active systems when the operation succeeds.

Did AI estimate the calories incorrectly?

A photo gives an estimate, not a measurement. Review foods and portions before saving; you can correct grams, calories, and macros. Oils, sauces, and portion sizes may not be visible in an image.

Why can’t I buy or restore a purchase?

In-app purchases are not available yet. You cannot buy or restore a HunterFit subscription until billing is configured and published in the relevant store.

Why don’t I see “Sign in with Apple”?

Apple sign-in will be enabled after the developer account, app, and authentication are configured for distribution.

What should I include when asking for help?

Briefly describe the steps, approximate time, device model, and app/system versions. Never share your password or a verification code.

AI features can make mistakes. Check estimates and consult a health professional for medical or personalized nutrition decisions.
